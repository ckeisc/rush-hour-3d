How to play

Your hero car is stuck in traffic. Slide the other vehicles out of the way and drive it through the EXIT gate on the right.

  • Drag any car or truck with the mouse (or your finger). It only slides forwards / backwards along its own direction.
  • Garage: swap in famous movie & TV cars — free the DeLorean, dodge KITT, the Batmobile, Ecto-1 and more.
  • Pass & play: pick 2–4 players in the ☰ menu — after every move, the turn (and the mouse!) passes to the next player.
  • Stuck? Tap Hint and the glowing car is your best next move.
